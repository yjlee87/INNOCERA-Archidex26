02 — The Material

Named for the mineral that gives it its silken skin.

Sericite is a fine, silvery silicate mica — prized in ceramics for a soft, pearl-matte lustre and a naturally warm, mineral touch. We took its name because it captures everything we want a bathroom surface to be: quiet underhand, resistant to glare, and honest about the material it's made from.

Every piece is developed and hand-finished by Innocera Holding, then glazed to hold that silken mineral character across years of daily use.

i.

Hand-finished

Each vessel is refined by hand before glazing — no two edges are machine-identical.

ii.

Mineral matte

A pearl-matte surface that softens light and resists fingerprints and glare.

iii.

Made to last

Dense, low-porosity glazing engineered for hygiene and a long, quiet life.
