Smart Fitness Mirror

50 × 150 cm · portrait, wall-mounted

A full-height mirror with a 32-inch touch screen living inside the glass. Dress in front of it, then follow a class on it — the same surface does both, because the screen only appears where the content is.

It runs Android 11 with the Play Store, so the room decides what it becomes: a trainer, a health record, a morning briefing, a speaker. The glass stays clear while it works, the LED edge shifts from warm to cool through the day, and IP44 sealing means it belongs in the bathroom, not next to it.

EO30-70 · Illustrative render
EO30-70

Holographic Advertising Mirror

Ø 70 cm · round, wall-mounted

A round copper-free mirror with a holographic fan built in behind the glass. Switched off, it is a mirror and nothing else. Switched on, an image assembles itself in the air in front of the surface — a rotating product, a logo, a message — with no visible screen to explain it.

Made for the places a mirror is already expected but attention has to be earned: retail floors, showrooms, hotel lifts, spa receptions. Bluetooth pairing handles the sound, and the supplied backboard carries the whole assembly on a single wall fixing.
