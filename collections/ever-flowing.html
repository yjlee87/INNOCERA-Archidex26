Ever Flowing · Water Closet Masterpieces

Still on the outside.
A vortex within.

Ever Flowing is our water closet collection — four pieces where all the engineering is hidden and all of it is the point. Rimless bowls swept by a spinning flush that reaches every millimetre of glaze, slim seats that close without a sound, and two integrated closets that open before you touch them.

Nothing about a water closet should ask for attention. Everything about this one earns it.

Water that never stops moving — and never leaves a trace.

The signature

Rimless Spinno.

A conventional bowl hides its water in a channel folded under the rim — the one surface in a bathroom nobody can reach and nothing can clean. Rimless removes the channel. The ledge is open all the way round, so what you can see is all there is.

Spinno then does the work the rim used to: water enters at a single point and is thrown into a vortex that sweeps the full circumference once before it drains. One entry, one rotation, complete coverage — on 4 litres, or 3 for a half flush.

  • i. Nothing folded away

    No rim cavity means no limescale shelf and no blind spot — a cloth reaches every part of the glaze in one pass.

  • ii. One entry, full sweep

    A single inlet aimed along the wall of the bowl turns the whole flush into one continuous rotation instead of a curtain of jets.

  • iii. Quieter, and thriftier

    Spinning water carries further than falling water, so the same clean happens on less of it — and with far less noise behind a bedroom wall.

The piece the whole collection is built toward. One unbroken ceramic shell — no cistern, no visible seam, no hardware asking to be looked at — with the entire interface reduced to a brushed seam under the lid and five points of light.

Integrated Water Closet

The same tankless silhouette brought down to a lower, longer line, with a single dial at the shoulder instead of a touch seam — a smart closet for rooms that would rather not look like they have one.

A 385 mm seat sits it slightly lower than a comfort-height pan, and the trapway is drawn to a 250 mm floor outlet, so it drops onto a standard S-trap rough-in without moving the drain.

One-Piece Water Closet
