The signature

Rimless Spinno.

A conventional bowl hides its water in a channel folded under the rim — the one surface in a bathroom nobody can reach and nothing can clean. Rimless removes the channel. The ledge is open all the way round, so what you can see is all there is.

Spinno then does the work the rim used to: water enters at a single point and is thrown into a vortex that sweeps the full circumference once before it drains. One entry, one rotation, complete coverage — on 4 litres, or 3 for a half flush.

  • i. Nothing folded away

    No rim cavity means no limescale shelf and no blind spot — a cloth reaches every part of the glaze in one pass.

  • ii. One entry, full sweep

    A single inlet aimed along the wall of the bowl turns the whole flush into one continuous rotation instead of a curtain of jets.

  • iii. Quieter, and thriftier

    Spinning water carries further than falling water, so the same clean happens on less of it — and with far less noise behind a bedroom wall.

Onefold Series
ONE10

One-Piece Water Closet

The low, folded silhouette of a smart closet with none of the dependency: ONE10 needs no electricity at all. No socket behind the pan, nothing to fail in a power cut, nothing to service.

The cistern is folded inside the one-piece form — a built-in tank rather than a direct-intake valve fed straight off the main. It fills, then flushes from its own stored head, so the flush is the same every time instead of rising and falling with the pressure in the street. 3.8 litres does it, and a bidet can be built in from the factory.
