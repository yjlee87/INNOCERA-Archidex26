Rainforest · Tap & Shower Head

Water, the way
a forest gives it.

Rainforest is our fittings collection — brass, chromed, and drawn down to two shapes: a flat lever that falls under the hand, and a fluted collar that gives the grip its texture.

Three basin mixers so far, one body language. Whichever height the basin asks for, the tap above it belongs to the same family.

  • CP brass
  • Fluted collar
  • Flat lever
  • Ceramic cartridge
  • Three heights

One handle, one collar, one family — three heights of water.

The detail

A collar you can feel.

Every mixer in the collection is cast in brass and chrome-plated, and every one carries the same two moves: a flat oval lever that sits low over the body, and a fluted collar turned into the cylinder beneath it.

The fluting is not decoration. It is the part of the tap a wet hand actually meets — fine vertical reeding gives grip where a polished cylinder gives none, and it hides the water marks that a mirror finish shows.

  • i. CP brass, not alloy

    A solid brass body under the chrome — heavier in the hand, and it takes a re-plate decades from now if it ever needs one.

  • ii. Ceramic cartridge

    The lever moves against a ceramic disc, so it stays the same weight to turn on the day it is installed and years after.

  • iii. Three heights, one face

    Under-counter, vessel, or a three-hole spread — specify by the basin, and the detailing stays identical across the room.

The standard of the family. A slim round column at counter height with the spout drawn straight out of it — the mixer for an inset or semi-recessed basin, where the water only has to fall a little way.

One lever does temperature and volume together, so a wet hand never has to find a second control.
